\section{Context}

Customer communications contain critical signals indicating potential business risks that require immediate attention. Early detection of security threats, legal issues, churn risks, and billing disputes is essential for protecting business operations, maintaining customer relationships, and ensuring regulatory compliance.

\subsection{Business Risk Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
\item Proactive identification of security breaches and fraud attempts requiring immediate escalation
\item Early warning system for legal and compliance issues to minimize organizational exposure
\item Detection of high-churn-risk customers for retention intervention programs
\item Identification of billing disputes to prevent customer dissatisfaction and regulatory issues
\item Real-time alerting capabilities for business-critical risk categories
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Operational Challenges}
\begin{itemize}
\item Manual risk identification is time-intensive and prone to human oversight
\item Risk signals may be buried within larger volumes of routine customer communications
\item Different risk types require different response timeframes and escalation procedures
\item False positive rates must be minimized to prevent alert fatigue
\item Visualization must communicate urgency and priority effectively to stakeholders
\end{itemize}

\section{Decision}

We implemented a \textbf{Keyword-Pattern-Based Risk Detection System} with categorical analysis and alert-themed visualization:

\subsection{Risk Detection Framework}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Four Critical Risk Categories}: Security \& Fraud, Legal \& Compliance, Urgent Churn Risk, Financial \& Billing Disputes
\item \textbf{Regex Pattern Matching}: Flexible keyword detection using regular expressions for comprehensive coverage
\item \textbf{Case-Insensitive Scanning}: Robust text analysis regardless of customer writing style
\item \textbf{Incident Aggregation}: Statistical counting of risk incidents by category for priority assessment
\item \textbf{Professional Alert Visualization}: Color-coded bar chart with incident counts and urgency indicators
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Risk Category Definitions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Security \& Fraud}: fraud|breach|hacked|vulnerability|phishing|unauthorized
\item \textbf{Legal \& Compliance}: legal|lawsuit|gdpr|compliance|sue|attorney
\item \textbf{Urgent Churn Risk}: cancel subscription|delete account|switch to competitor|closing my account|frustrated|angry
\item \textbf{Financial \& Billing Disputes}: overcharge|double charge|incorrect bill|dispute charge|refund duplicate
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Alert System Design}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Priority Ordering}: Risk categories sorted by incident frequency for immediate attention focus
\item \textbf{Visual Alert Theme}: YlOrRd\_r color palette creating urgency gradient from yellow to red
\item \textbf{Incident Labeling}: Clear numeric incident counts with "Incidents" suffix for stakeholder clarity
\item \textbf{Professional Layout}: Business radar theme with subtitle explanation and clean typography
\end{itemize}

\section{Rationale}

\subsection{Why Keyword-Pattern Detection}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Immediate Implementation}: No training data or model development required for rapid deployment
\item \textbf{High Precision}: Well-chosen keywords provide reliable signal detection with minimal false positives
\item \textbf{Transparency}: Risk detection logic is explainable and auditable for compliance requirements
\item \textbf{Customizability}: Pattern sets easily updated based on emerging risk trends and business needs
\item \textbf{Performance}: Regex matching provides fast, scalable analysis for real-time alerting
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Business Risk Coverage}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Security Protection}: Early detection of fraud and security incidents minimizes damage scope
\item \textbf{Legal Liability}: Proactive identification of legal issues enables rapid response and mitigation
\item \textbf{Customer Retention}: Churn risk detection enables targeted intervention and retention programs
\item \textbf{Financial Protection}: Billing dispute identification prevents escalation and regulatory issues
\item \textbf{Operational Excellence}: Systematic risk monitoring improves overall business resilience
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Alternative Approaches Rejected}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Machine Learning Classification}: Requires labeled training data and longer development cycles
\item \textbf{Sentiment Analysis Only}: Too broad and misses specific risk indicators
\item \textbf{Manual Review Processes}: Scale limitations and inconsistent human detection rates
\item \textbf{Third-Party Risk APIs}: Data privacy concerns and dependency on external services
\item \textbf{Complex NLP Pipelines}: Over-engineering for pattern detection requirements
\end{itemize}

\section{Implementation Details}

\subsection{Risk Detection Algorithm}
\begin{align}
\text{Risk Categories} &= \{\text{Security}, \text{Legal}, \text{Churn}, \text{Billing}\} \\
\text{Pattern Match} &: \text{df['text'].str.contains}(\text{pattern}, \text{case=False}) \\
\text{Incident Count} &= \sum(\text{Pattern Matches per Category}) \\
\text{Risk Priority} &= \text{sort\_values}(\text{incident\_count}, \text{ascending=False})
\end{align}
